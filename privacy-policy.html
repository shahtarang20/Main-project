Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how QuickCalc ("we", "our", "us") handles information when you visit quickcalc-app.vercel.app (the "Site"). We built QuickCalc to work entirely in your browser, so we collect far less information than most websites — but this page explains exactly what does happen.

1. Information You Enter Into Calculators

Every calculator on this Site runs using client-side JavaScript. Values you type (loan amounts, dates of birth, height, weight, income figures, etc.) are processed locally in your browser and are never transmitted to, or stored on, our servers. We have no database and no backend — once you close or refresh the page, that data is gone.

2. Currency Converter Exception

Our currency converter tool sends the currency codes you select (e.g. "USD", "INR") to a third-party exchange-rate API in order to fetch current rates. No personally identifiable information is included in this request.

3. Cookies and Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on this Site. Google and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to collect information about your visits to this and other websites in order to provide advertisements about goods and services of interest to you. This may include the use of your web browsing history to help determine, predict and understand your behaviors and interests.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also visit www.aboutads.info to opt out of third-party vendor use of cookies for personalized advertising.

4. Analytics

We may use analytics services (such as Google Analytics) to understand aggregate traffic patterns — for example, which pages are most visited. These services may use cookies and collect information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, and pages visited. This data is aggregated and not used to personally identify you.

5. Children's Privacy

This Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR (EU) or CCPA (California) regarding data collected by advertising partners. Since QuickCalc itself does not collect or store personal data, requests regarding ad personalization should be directed to Google via the links above.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit our Contact page.
