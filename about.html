About QuickCalc

QuickCalc was created with a simple goal: give anyone, anywhere in the world, free and instant access to the everyday calculators they need — without signing up, without installing an app, and without their data ever leaving their own browser.

Whether you're a student checking a percentage, a parent tracking a child's age, someone planning a home loan, or a small business owner estimating taxes, our tools are designed to be simple, accurate, and fast.

Why we built this

Many calculator websites are cluttered with pop-ups, require account creation, or load slowly. We wanted something different: clean pages, clear results, and calculations that happen instantly in your browser using standard, well-tested formulas.

How our tools work

All calculations on QuickCalc run entirely on your device using JavaScript. We do not store, transmit, or have access to any values you enter — nothing is saved on a server because we don't use one. The only exception is our currency converter, which fetches publicly available exchange rate data from a third-party API to display up-to-date conversion rates.

Our commitment

We aim to keep every tool free, ad-supported, and accessible to everyone regardless of location or device. If you have suggestions for new tools or spot an issue with an existing one, please reach out via our Contact page.
