About Our Exchange Rates

This converter fetches live exchange rate data from a public exchange rate API when you click "Convert." Rates are updated regularly by the data provider but may lag slightly behind the real-time interbank rates used by banks or payment processors, and typically do not include the markup or fees a bank or money transfer service would charge on an actual transaction.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the rate I see different from my bank's rate?

Banks and money transfer services add a margin on top of the mid-market rate shown here, and their rates update on their own schedule. This tool is best used to get a quick reference point, not the exact rate you'll receive from a provider.

Do I need an internet connection for this tool?

Yes — unlike our other calculators, currency conversion requires fetching live rates from an external data source, so it needs an active internet connection.

Which currencies are supported?

We currently support 15 major world currencies including USD, EUR, GBP, INR, JPY, AUD, and more, with additional currencies planned.
