How GST Calculation Works

GST (Goods and Services Tax) is added to the base price of goods and services in India at rates typically of 5%, 12%, 18%, or 28% depending on the item category. To add GST, multiply the base amount by the rate and add it to the base. To remove GST from a GST-inclusive total, divide the total by (1 + rate/100) to find the base price, then subtract that from the total to find the GST portion.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between CGST, SGST, and IGST?

For intra-state transactions, GST is split equally into CGST (Central) and SGST (State). For inter-state transactions, IGST applies as a single combined tax. This calculator shows the total GST amount only — check with your accountant for the CGST/SGST split.

Which GST rate should I use?

GST rates vary by product/service category as set by the GST Council. Check the official GST rate schedule for your specific item.

Is this suitable for GST return filing?

This tool is for quick estimation only. For actual GST returns and compliance, use official GST portal tools or consult a tax professional.
