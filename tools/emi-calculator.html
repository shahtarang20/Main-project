How EMI Is Calculated

EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) is the fixed amount you pay each month toward a loan until it's fully repaid. It's calculated using the formula EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n − 1), where P is the loan principal, r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100), and n is the total number of monthly installments (loan tenure in years × 12). Each EMI payment includes both principal repayment and interest, with the interest portion typically higher in early months and gradually decreasing over the loan term.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a higher tenure always mean I pay more overall?

Yes — while a longer tenure lowers your monthly EMI, it increases the total interest paid over the life of the loan because interest accrues for a longer period.

Is this EMI calculator accurate for all loan types?

It works for standard fixed-rate loans such as home, car, and personal loans. Loans with floating rates, processing fees, or prepayment penalties may have a different effective cost — check with your lender for exact figures.

Can I use this for any currency?

Yes, the formula is currency-independent — simply enter your loan amount in your local currency and the result will be in the same currency.
