About This Converter

This tool converts between common units of length, weight, and temperature using standard, internationally recognized conversion factors (for example, 1 mile = 1609.34 meters, 1 pound = 0.453592 kilograms). Temperature conversions use the standard Celsius, Fahrenheit, and Kelvin formulas rather than a simple multiplication factor, since temperature scales have different zero points.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are the conversions?

Conversions use standard, widely accepted conversion constants and are accurate to four decimal places, suitable for everyday, educational, and most professional uses.

Why is temperature conversion handled differently?

Celsius, Fahrenheit, and Kelvin scales have different zero points and step sizes, so converting between them requires a formula (e.g., °F = °C × 9/5 + 32) rather than a simple multiplier used for length or weight.

Can I add more unit categories?

We plan to add volume, speed, and area conversions in future updates based on user demand.
