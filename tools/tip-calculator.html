How Tipping Works

This calculator finds the tip amount by multiplying your bill by the tip percentage, then divides the total (bill + tip) evenly among the number of people specified, giving you the per-person amount to pay. Standard tipping norms vary by country and service type (e.g., 15-20% at restaurants in the US, often optional or included as a service charge elsewhere) — adjust the percentage to match local custom.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's a typical tip percentage?

In the US, 15-20% is common for restaurant service. In many other countries, tipping norms are lower or a service charge may already be included in the bill — check local custom before tipping.

Can I split unevenly among people?

This calculator splits the total evenly. For itemized or uneven splits based on what each person ordered, you'd need to calculate each person's share manually.
