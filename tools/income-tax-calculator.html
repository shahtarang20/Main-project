How This Calculator Works

This tool estimates tax under India's new (default) tax regime for FY 2025-26, which uses simplified slabs without most deductions and exemptions available under the old regime. Income up to ₹7,00,000 is effectively tax-free due to the rebate under Section 87A, and a 4% Health & Education Cess is added on top of the computed tax. Tax slabs and rebates are set by the government and can change each financial year — always check the latest notification from the Income Tax Department or consult a tax professional before filing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this include deductions like 80C or HRA?

No — the new regime this calculator uses generally does not allow most common deductions. If you're on the old regime with deductions, your actual liability will differ from this estimate.

Is this calculator only for India?

Yes, currently this tool is built around India's new tax regime slabs. We plan to add calculators for other countries in the future.

Is this legally binding or official?

No. This is an educational estimate only. Please refer to official government sources or a qualified chartered accountant for actual tax filing.
