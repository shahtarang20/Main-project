How This Estimate Works

CTC (Cost to Company) includes your base salary plus employer contributions and benefits, which are not part of your actual take-home pay. This calculator applies a simplified model: it estimates a basic salary portion of CTC, deducts employee provident fund (PF) contribution, applies an approximate income tax based on our tax calculator's slabs, and subtracts any other deductions you specify, to arrive at an estimated monthly in-hand salary.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my actual take-home different from this estimate?

Actual salary structures vary widely between companies — components like HRA, special allowances, gratuity, bonuses, and professional tax differ by employer and city. This tool provides a general approximation, not an exact payslip calculation.

Does this include professional tax?

No — professional tax varies by Indian state (typically ₹200/month or less) and is not included in this estimate.

What PF percentage should I use?

The standard employee PF contribution in India is 12% of basic salary. Check your payslip or HR policy for your exact contribution rate.
