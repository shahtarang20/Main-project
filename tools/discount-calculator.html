How Discounts Are Calculated

The final sale price is found by subtracting the discount amount (original price × discount percentage) from the original price. This is one of the most common everyday percentage calculations, useful when shopping, comparing sale offers, or estimating savings during festival and seasonal sales.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate stacked discounts (e.g. 20% + 10%)?

Stacked discounts are applied sequentially, not added together. Calculate the first discount, then apply the second discount to the already-discounted price — run this calculator twice for an accurate result.

Does this include tax?

No — this shows the discounted price only. Use our GST Calculator to add applicable tax on top of the discounted price.
