Percentage Formulas Explained

This page covers the three most common percentage questions people search for: finding what a percentage of a number is (X% of Y), finding what percentage one number is of another (X is what % of Y), and finding the percentage change between two values. These formulas are useful for everything from calculating discounts and tips to analyzing exam scores and business growth.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a discount using this tool?

Use the first calculator: enter the discount percentage and the original price to find the discount amount, then subtract it from the original price for the final cost.

What's the difference between percentage change and percentage points?

Percentage change (calculated here) measures relative change between two values. Percentage points measure the absolute difference between two percentages — a distinct calculation not covered by this tool.

Can I use negative numbers?

The "From/To" percentage change calculator supports negative changes (decreases) automatically — it will indicate whether the result is an increase or decrease.
