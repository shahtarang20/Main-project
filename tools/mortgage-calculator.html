How Mortgage Payments Are Calculated

Your monthly mortgage payment is made up of Principal & Interest (P&I), calculated from your loan amount (home price minus down payment), interest rate, and term, plus an estimated monthly share of annual property tax and homeowners insurance. This calculator does not include private mortgage insurance (PMI) or HOA fees, which may apply depending on your down payment size and property type.

Frequently Asked Questions

What down payment do I need to avoid PMI?

In many markets, a down payment of at least 20% of the home price avoids private mortgage insurance. This calculator does not add PMI automatically — factor it in separately if your down payment is below that threshold.

Does this include closing costs?

No — closing costs are one-time fees paid at purchase and are not part of the recurring monthly payment estimated here.

Why is my estimate different from my lender's quote?

Lenders factor in your credit score, exact loan product, PMI, HOA fees, and local tax rates, which can shift the real payment. Use this as a starting estimate, not a final quote.
