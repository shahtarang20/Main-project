How Age Is Calculated

This tool calculates your exact age by comparing your date of birth to today's date, breaking down the difference into complete years, months, and days rather than a rough estimate. It correctly accounts for varying month lengths and leap years, so the result reflects your precise age at this moment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this account for leap years?

Yes — the calculation uses actual calendar dates, so leap years and varying month lengths are automatically handled correctly.

Can I calculate age as of a future or past date instead of today?

Currently this tool compares your date of birth to today's date only. A "calculate as of custom date" option may be added in the future.

Is my date of birth stored anywhere?

No. The date you enter is processed entirely in your browser and is never sent to or stored on any server.
